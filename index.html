DuoSound Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

DuoSound is a Windows app that plays your PC's sound on several Bluetooth earbuds or speakers at the same time. This page explains what DuoSound does with your data. In short: everything stays on your PC.

What DuoSound does with your data

  • PC audio: DuoSound copies the sound your PC is playing (or, in "One app only" mode, the sound of the one app you pick) to the devices you choose. The audio is processed in memory on your PC and is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Microphone: only while you run Auto-sync, DuoSound listens to your microphone for a few seconds to hear a short test beep from each device and measure its delay. The recording stays in memory, is thrown away right after the measurement, and is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Settings: your device choices, volumes, delays and presets are saved in a file on your PC so DuoSound remembers them. A small log file on your PC records errors to help fix problems. Neither file leaves your PC unless you choose to send it.

What DuoSound does NOT do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • DuoSound itself never connects to the internet. (Once, after you've used it a few times, it may show the Microsoft Store's own "Rate this app" window. That window is run by Microsoft and only sends a rating if you choose to leave one.)
  • Nothing is shared with the developer or any third party.
